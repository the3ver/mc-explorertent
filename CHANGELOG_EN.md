# Changelog

## [0.1.0] - 2026-11-08

### Added
- The explorer tent from WorldExplorer as the standalone mod `explorertent`.
- Worlds and inventories from WorldExplorer up to 0.12 keep loading: the old `mc3ver:*` tent ids point to `explorertent:*`.

### Notes
- Refuses to start next to WorldExplorer 0.12 or older. WorldExplorer 0.13.0 and newer ships this mod itself.
- Recipe book progress for the tent recipes is lost on the switch.
