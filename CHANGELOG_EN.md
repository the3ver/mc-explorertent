# Changelog

## [0.1.3] - 2026-11-28

### Fixed

- Since Minecraft 26.3 rain fell through the whole tent down to the floor. The tent canvas keeps rain and snow out again.

## [0.1.2] - 2026-11-27

### Fixed

- Rain and snow no longer fall into the roof peak under the ridge when standing inside the tent.

## [0.1.1] - 2026-11-08

### Fixed
- The mod file is named `explorertent-<version>.jar` instead of `mc-explorertent-<version>.jar`.
- The description now says that the furnace drops its contents when the tent is packed.

## [0.1.0] - 2026-11-08

### Added
- The explorer tent from WorldExplorer as the standalone mod `explorertent`.
- Worlds and inventories from WorldExplorer up to 0.12 keep loading: the old `mc3ver:*` tent ids point to `explorertent:*`.

### Notes
- Refuses to start next to WorldExplorer 0.12 or older. WorldExplorer 0.13.0 and newer ships this mod itself.
- Recipe book progress for the tent recipes is lost on the switch.
